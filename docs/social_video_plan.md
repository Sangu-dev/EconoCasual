## 2. Video idea (proposal, confirm in the group)

Goal: explain in simple words why a discount should go to the right customers, and show the dashboard.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|------|------|---------|
| 1. Hook | 0 to 8 s | "A customer who would have bought anyway still got a discount. Money wasted." |
| 2. Idea | 8 to 25 s | Causal ML estimates how much a discount changes each customer's spending. We call high responders Persuadables. |
| 3. What we built | 25 to 45 s | Short screen clips: upload page, uplift chart, customer table. |
| 4. Result | 45 to 55 s | Optimized plan vs blanket discount, from the dashboard. Use real numbers only after the optimizer results exist. |
| 5. Close | 55 to 60 s | Team Group 25, Infotact Solutions internship, project name. |

Mid review version: beats 1 to 3 only, since optimizer results are not ready yet.
